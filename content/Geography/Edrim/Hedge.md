Hedge is a small village south of the Eldorath Mountains that began as an outpost of the Verdant Hand's expansion.  The original druid settler left the cover of the forest and founded hedge as a base to continue the growth of the forest, reclaiming territory with new growth.  The hedge itself was grown to mark boundaries of the village, and individual properties.  As the town has grown, new portions of the hedge are grown to match.  

In the year 823, Callum Crane, the Druid of Hedge died unexpectedly, and without a successor, leaving Hedge without a former governor.
# Population

Hedge is populated by a wide mix of people.  While many heritages are visible in Hedge, they are often limited to a single family unit or two.  Urchin and Vulpin families make up the bulk of the populace, as they were some of the first settlers, and their families have expanded in the village over several generations.  
# Government

In the tradition of the Verdant Hand, Hedge is lead by a Druid who has passed the Verdant Trial.
