Edrim is a diverse landscape populated by a large variation of societies, many of which follow the old ways, rejecting the modern pantheon and remained loyal to the elemental forces.  No single power controls Edrim, and instead it is governed by a collection of city states each with ties to druidic traditions and worship of the elemental powers.  Edrim is filled with wild place, and unusual magic bleeds into the material plane.

# Society

The Druidic Orders of Edrim govern the continent forming a government comprised of representative members from each Order.  The individual orders are responsible for choosing a single representative to hold station on the Primordial Council.  

In addition, each order maintains some for of contest or trial to become a full member of the Order, granting additional privilege for those who have succeeded 

It is said that should a Druid ever complete all 5 trials set forth by the orders, they would gain the blessing of the Primordials.  However, there is no recorded history of this being accomplished.  

## The Verdant Hand 

The Verdant Hand comprise of followers of Kren, the Growth-Warden.  Three sects exists within the Verdant Hand, those that live on the northern shore of Edrim, amongst the tropical jungles and those that live south of the Eldoath Moutains, deep within the Deciduous forests, and those that live far to the southwest, in the boreal forests of Edrim.  

The Verdant Hand strives for growth.  tending the natural spaces of the world with the goal of proliferating the natural growth of the world, both plant and animal.  

## The Veiled Grotto
Druids of The Veiled Grotto follow Nomon, the Everdepth.  They are widespread throughout Edrim, with their settlements built on or adjacent to bodies of water.  a subset of the Veiled Grotto aspire to be able to live wholly within the water.  looking for magical means to either change their forms, or submerge their settlements to accomplish this goal.  

## The Grove of Embers

The Grove of Embers understands that destruction is a necessity for growth, and are followers of  Cendis, The Ashen.  They believe that the destructive powers of fire and magma are vital to the generation of new lands, as well as purging overgrowth in order to allow new life to take hold.  

Often at odds with the Verdant Hand, the Grove of Embers strikes balance in the land preventing unmitigated growth.

## The Zephyr Circle 

The Zephyr Circle are followers of Altus, The Nine Winds, living primarily amongst the Eldorath Mountains.  Druids of the Zephyr circle undertake a ritual in order to attain higher station within the circle.  Disciples of Altus climb the various named peaks within the Eldorath Mountains, and, trusting in the winds to protect them, throw themselves from the peaks.  Ones station is determined by the highest of the peaks a disciple has jumped from and survived.  

Those that fail the trials are deemed unworthy of the Primordial's blessing.
## The Ring of Rot

The Ring of Rot do not have a region or central home, but instead function as a nomadic people within Edrim and beyond.  worshipping Mycon, The Returned; the Ring travel the lands performing rights of rebirth, 

Others view the Ring with suspicion, while also recognizing their necessity.
