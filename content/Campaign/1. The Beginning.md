It is the year 823 by the reckoning of the Keepers of Imeron.  The gods of Galerest have been absent from the world since their reported deaths eight centuries prior in the events of The Fallow.  Their corpses sit as monuments to their power at The Seat of the Gods.  But the belief in them did not die with their physical forms.  True believers are said to still be able to draw power from those beings, and even hear their whispers reaching out from where they rest.    

The peoples of [Edrim](content/Geography/Edrim/Edrim.md) have returned to the worship of the Primordial Beings that existed in the time before records.  While absent from the world, their influence can still be discovered and channeled by the Elemental Druids.  

In [Imeron](content/Geography/Imeron/Imeron.md), knights quest for glory and the favor of the nobility.  Promising their strength and bravery to support the kingdom, and their gods.  

The wastes of [Velenar](content/Geography/Velenar/Velenar.md) are dotted with pockets of luxury.  Towns and cities built into the few habitable areas remaining on the continent, a relic of a long forgotten war. 

Ships patrol the waters of [Kalas](content/Geography/Kalas/Kalas.md), where an burgeoning war between the Siren Song Confederacy and the Toria Trade Company threaten to spill into the rest of Galerest as territory and trading rights expand and are fought over.


You find yourself in the village of [Hedge](content/Geography/Edrim/Hedge.md), a small settlement of approximately 500 residents, named for the waist high hedge wall surrounding much of the town.  Hedge is situated on the outer edge of a territory ruled by The Verdant Hand.  

It is a time of celebration, as a druid of The Ring of Rot has arrived to perform the rites of passing for an elder who had recently passed.  People from surrounding villages have come to Hedge to pay their respects and celebrate the life of one so revered, bringing with them food music and games.  The village has surged to almost twice its size as travelers have set a makeshift encampment outside the hedge.

The rites are due to be performed in 3 days time, at the next full moon.  However word has reached each of you that something is awry, and that the Druid of the Ring of Rot is seeking adventurers to assist in preparations for the rites.  

And that is where we find each of you, in Hedge for your individual reasons, but summoned to the same place to discuss an opportunity to work.
